## The portfolio

Six to ten concurrent software implementation projects at any given time, each valued
between **$75K and $250K** — about **$1.5M annually**. Teams are matrixed: two to three
engineers per project, none of them my direct reports.

Clients are primarily **state agencies and county governments**, with some private-sector
work in manufacturing, HR, and education.

## Public-sector delivery is a different job

Implementations for government live and die on client-side approval cycles that no vendor
controls. I manage that by front-loading dependency mapping, building explicit
client-task milestones into the baseline, and escalating early when an approval slips.

Budget is the variable I *can* control, and I hold **95%+ on budget** across the
portfolio.

The clearest example: a **state regulatory agency implementation delivered ahead of
schedule and on budget**, which came down to disciplined risk, status, and budget
communication and a strong engineering team.

## Beyond delivery

Drove **AI tool adoption into the department's PM workflow** and built budget and
timeline tracking tools now used department-wide — an estimated 20–30% improvement in
throughput for the analysts using them.

Day to day, execution is tracked in Azure DevOps, and I own QA test case design and
acceptance criteria through development evaluation.
