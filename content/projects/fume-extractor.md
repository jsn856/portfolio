The [Pinecil case](/projects/pinecil-case/) came first. Once I had an iron I enjoyed using,
I used it more, and the smoke stopped being something I could wave away.

Most bench extractors assume a bench. Mine assumes a DeWalt pack, so it goes where the work
is instead of tying me to an outlet.

## Where this sits

I could not solder at all a few years ago. I learned at work, built a case for the iron,
built this once the fumes became the problem, and then took on a
[split keyboard](/projects/split-keyboard/) with a few hundred joints in it.
