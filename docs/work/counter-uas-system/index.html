Why protocol takeover matters

The system presented itself to a drone as its new controller rather than jamming its signal. That distinction is the whole product.

RF jamming is heavily restricted for domestic US use. Non-jamming protocol takeover is the commercially and legally viable approach, and it's the reason a counter-drone system can be fielded somewhere with civilians in it at all. Anyone working in this space understands the difference immediately.

The role

Employee #3. The company went from 4 to 50 people and raised $14M+ during my time there.

I directed engineering operations — development strategy, scheduling, scope planning, and financial management — across a ~$400K annual engineering budget with two direct reports. I also ran technical due diligence for fundraising, which in practice meant owning whether the product demo worked when investors were in the room.

Implemented the company's first ERP/MRP system, which gave us material and inventory tracking we hadn't had and measurably improved manufacturing reliability.

On the title

Director of Engineering Operations was a generous title for a first job out of college, and I'd rather say so than have someone wonder. Employee #3 at a company that reached 50 people and raised $14M is the part that actually means something.
