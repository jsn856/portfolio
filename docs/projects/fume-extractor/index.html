Fume Extractor

A portable solder fume extractor that runs off DeWalt battery packs, built once soldering stopped being an occasional chore.

  • 3D Printing
  • Electronics
  • Fusion 360

This case study is still an outline — the facts below are verified, the writing isn't finished.

The Pinecil case came first. Once I had an iron I enjoyed using, I used it more, and the smoke stopped being something I could wave away.

Most bench extractors assume a bench. Mine assumes a DeWalt pack, so it goes where the work is instead of tying me to an outlet.

Where this sits

I could not solder at all a few years ago. I learned at work, built a case for the iron, built this once the fumes became the problem, and then took on a split keyboard with a few hundred joints in it.
